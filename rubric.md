## How to score

Score every product on every category from 0 to 10. Use half points. Use the same strict scale for every product:

- 10: best in class. You would not change anything.
- 9: excellent. Only small nits remain.
- 8: good, with a few clear problems.
- 7: solid but generic, or several problems.
- 5: obvious problems that hurt use.
- 3 or lower: broken or confusing.

Judge each product across all of its screens and themes together. Most real products land between 6 and 9. Do not inflate scores. Look at the products side by side before you score any of them.

## Issues

For every product, list the changes you would make, most severe first, at most 6 per product. Each issue is one change. The team makes each change and marks it on the next screenshots, so be precise. For each issue give:

- the product letter, the screen, and the category,
- where: the section and the element, precise enough to find, for example "basket panel, the Remove link under each line",
- the severity,
- the problem: what is wrong,
- the fix: one concrete change, with values when you can: sizes, weights, colors, spacing, radii.

Do not list praise. Do not list the same change twice.
