## How to compare

Compare the two versions screen by screen. Look at hierarchy, typography, color and contrast, spacing and layout, consistency, iconography, and how clearly interactive elements read as interactive.

For each screen, pick the better version: "X" or "Y". Answer "tie" only when you cannot see a difference in quality. Then pick the better version overall, across all screens. Give a one-sentence reason for every pick. In your text, always write "Version X" or "Version Y", never the letter alone. Do not assume that either version is the newer one.
